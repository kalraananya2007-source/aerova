# Aerova – Problem Statement

Airports face challenges in managing aircraft schedules, runway allocation, gate assignments, and ground crew availability. We aim to develop **Aerova**, a Java-based airport operations simulator that automates resource allocation, detects scheduling conflicts, prioritizes emergency flights, and improves coordination and operational efficiency.

## Project Objective

To simulate airport ground operations and demonstrate how scheduling, resource allocation, and conflict detection can improve coordination between aircraft, runways, gates, and ground crew.
