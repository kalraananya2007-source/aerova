# Team Work Division

## Member 1 – Aircraft & Flight Management
- Manage aircraft and flight records.
- Add, update, delete, and validate flight details.
- Track flight status.
- Main concepts: classes, objects, encapsulation, inheritance, constructors, ArrayList, HashMap, exceptions, JDBC.

## Member 2 – Runway Scheduling & Priority Management
- Manage runway availability.
- Schedule arrivals and departures.
- Prioritize emergency flights and detect runway conflicts.
- Main concepts: PriorityQueue, Comparator, Collections, interfaces, multithreading, ExecutorService, synchronization, exceptions.

## Member 3 – Gate Allocation, Ground Crew & Conflict Management
- Assign gates and ground crew.
- Check resource availability and detect conflicting assignments.
- Suggest alternative gates and generate alerts.
- Main concepts: OOP, ArrayList, HashMap, enums, synchronization, multithreading, custom exceptions, JDBC.

## Member 4 – GUI, Database Integration & Reports
- Build the JavaFX dashboard and alert views.
- Integrate the modules and database.
- Generate operational reports and export CSV files.
- Main concepts: JavaFX, event handling, JDBC, SQL, DAO, exceptions, file handling, Collections.

## Shared Team Responsibilities
- Agree on model classes and service method signatures before parallel development.
- Use feature branches and pull requests.
- Review and test integrated changes.
- Every member should understand the complete application and core Java concepts.
